Greetings, Traveler!

It appears that you might not have found this repository in the shape or condition you were hoping for. Or maybe you're a hater, and you do wish to see us in this state.

Nevertheless, if you'd like to get our source code, it's in the releases. If you have time, are bored, or are generally interested, you can indulge in the history of the (partial) empire that once was Crafted Gamz.

I acquired Crafted Gamz when I was in 6th grade. It was created by a friend of mine as a simple game launcher. It had no styling. It was just square and white. At that point, it was called LJH Web Games v2.26. It had around 15 games. But my stupid 6th-grade mind was still mesmerized by it.

I started pushing him to update it, and update it, and update it. He eventually let me fork and adopt it. I started coding it with ChatGPT (I had no experience), and over the next year or two I added custom backgrounds, tons of games, a weird proxy, and other stuff.

I moved the versions all the way up to v10, based on Drive Games' infrastructure. Then I decided to code my own.

v11 came, and it was amazing. Then came v12, and the UI was beautiful. Then v13, featuring Liquid Glass (inspired by vplaza). v14 was a complete redesign built from the ground up. Finally came v15.

v15 introduced an onboarding experience, a desktop mode, a website mode (v14), and a browser mode. It was beautiful. It reached millions of users.

But then a feeling started to creep in.

I felt bored, and I knew I had to do something else.

That's when I made a drastic change.

I stopped Crafted Gamz.

Today, July 18, 2026, I officially discontinued Crafted Gamz. I know I needed to do something more, and I'm working on it. I apologize for this, but all good things eventually come to an end.

So farewell, Traveler.

I hope this story made your everlasting exploration of the vastness of the internet at least a little more enjoyable.
