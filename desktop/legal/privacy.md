# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: April 11, 2026**

---

Estimated Read: 2 Minutes

---

Crafted Gamz is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

**Account information**: When you register, we collect your name, email address, and a hashed password. We recommend using an Oauth provider, as this does not give you a password.\n\n

**Game data**: We sync your localStorage data (game progress, settings, preferences) to our servers so it is available across devices.\n\n

**OAuth data**: If you sign in with Google or GitHub, we receive your name, email, and profile photo from that provider.

## 2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

- Provide and maintain the sync service
- Restore your game progress across devices
- Identify your account and authenticate you securely
- Sync your exact state through devices

We do **not** sell your data to third parties.

We will only dispense your info is requird by law.

## 3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore. Firebase's infrastructure is governed by Google's security and compliance standards.

## 4. Cookies and Local Storage

We use localStorage to store your game state locally on your device. This data is mirrored to our servers when you are signed in.

## 5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third party services in relation to your data:

- **Firebase (by Google)** — authentication and database (for the user data and global data)
- **GitHub OAuth** — optional sign-in provider
- **Google OAuth** — optional sign-in provider

## 6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time through the far right button on the top bar > settings > data > delete. Upon deletion, your Firestore document and all synced storage data will be permanently removed. You can also delete all your data but not your account, as well as export your data in the same area.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Crafted Gamz is not directed at children under 11. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 11.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We are not required to notify users of changes.

## 9. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, please contact us by emailing crafted@craftedgamz.com.
